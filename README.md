### 🚀 Business Solutions Portfolio

#### 1. [Retail Inventory Management & Demand Analytics](https://github.com/karty123/Inventory_Management_Analytics)
* **Business Context:** A retail supply chain was facing systemic overstocking, leading to inefficiently tied-up capital and blind forecasting.
* **Technical Execution:** Developed an automated ETL pipeline using Excel Power Query to process and standardize 73K+ store-product records.
* **Measurable Impact:** Designed logic-based KPIs and forecast accuracy models to continuously flag overstocked items, providing a concrete framework to optimize inventory flow.

#### 2. [Food Delivery Operations Analytics](https://github.com/karty123/Food_Delivery_Operations_Analytics)
* **Business Context:** Unstructured logistical data was obscuring delivery bottlenecks and unit profitability across the network.
* **Technical Execution:** Architected a comprehensive Star Schema database from the ground up to manage 149K+ delivery records.
* **Measurable Impact:** Implemented DAX-driven financial tracking and reconciliation views in Power BI, delivering precise mathematical visibility into unit economics.

#### 3. [Customer Retention & Revenue Risk Matrix](https://github.com/karty123/customer_churn_sql_business_intelligence)
* **Business Context:** Inconsistent internal logic for defining customer drop-off was preventing proactive retention strategies.
* **Technical Execution:** Processed 100K+ orders across 90K+ unique customers using advanced SQL to fully standardize churn logic.
* **Measurable Impact:** Built dynamic retention cohorts that quantified at-risk revenue, allowing stakeholders to target high-value drop-offs with concrete business intelligence.
